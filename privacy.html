Privacy Policy

Who we are

Early Phoenix is a data intelligence consultancy based in the United Kingdom. This website is operated by Early Phoenix. Our contact email is kim@earlyphoenix.uk.

What data we collect

This website is a static site and does not use cookies, tracking pixels, or any form of user authentication. We do not collect personal data through this website except when you voluntarily contact us by email.

If you contact us, we may retain your name and email address for the purpose of responding to your enquiry.

Data we collect from public marketplaces

To produce market intelligence for animal welfare charities, researchers, local authorities and businesses, Early Phoenix collects adverts that are publicly listed on UK online marketplaces, including adverts for animals for sale.

  • What we collect: the details shown in a public advert, such as the animal’s breed, age and price, the platform, the date, the location the seller gives (usually town and county), the seller’s display name or platform ID, and the advert text.
  • How: from public pages only, without logging in or creating accounts, at a limited rate, and respecting each site’s robots.txt.
  • Why, and our lawful basis: our legitimate interests, and those of our clients, in understanding the online market for animal welfare, research, licensing and business purposes (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(f)). We have assessed that these interests are not overridden by sellers’ rights, and keep that assessment on file.
  • What we share: our reports and dashboards show aggregate figures. We do not sell personal data. Where a local authority that licenses animal sellers asks for seller-level information for its regulatory work, we share it only under a written agreement.
  • How long we keep it: seller names, seller IDs and advert text for up to 24 months, after which they are deleted or replaced with a code. Aggregate figures are kept.
  • Your rights: if you think an advert of yours is in our data, you can ask to see it, object to our use of it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing kim@earlyphoenix.uk. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.
