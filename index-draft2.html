Method

Why it holds up

The outputs are built to be forwarded: to a board, a minister, a trustee. 3 rules make that safe.

1. The market is joined to the rule that governs it

Listings are reconciled against the councils' licence registers, and reptile listings against the protected-species lists. That is what turns a market view into a case: the same seller across platforms, a licence that has expired, a chip that is missing.

2. Named, published measures

Demand Signal is a named metric with a published definition, window and caveat, used the same way in every deck. When a number moves, it is the same number that moved last month.

3. Provenance on every output

Snapshot date, base size and suppression rule on every chart. Small numbers are suppressed. Nothing is estimated. Every figure can be traced back to the month it was collected.
